About the Island

Small Island, a Lot Going On

Taniti covers less than 500 square miles. White sandy beaches, a natural harbor, rainforest, and an active volcano — genuinely different terrain within a short drive of each other.

About 20,000 Tanitians live here. The island has been welcoming tourists for years and has the restaurants, lodging, and activities to show for it.

Travel Guide

Plan Your Trip to Taniti

Transportation, currency, health, and safety — what you need to know before you land.

Getting to Taniti

Arriving on the Island

Most visitors arrive by air. A weekly cruise ship also stops at Yellow Leaf Bay harbor.

✈️

By Air

Taniti's small airport accommodates jets and propeller planes. Expansion for larger jets is underway.

🚢

By Cruise Ship

A small cruise ship docks in Yellow Leaf Bay harbor once per week for a one-night stay.

Getting Around

Island Transportation

Taniti City is flat and very walkable. Multiple options are available for exploring the rest of the island.

🚌

Public Bus

Serves Taniti City daily, 5 a.m.–11 p.m. Private buses serve the rest of the island.

🚗

Rental Car

Available from a local agency near the airport. Best option for exploring the whole island.

🚕

Taxi

Taxis are available throughout Taniti City for quick, convenient rides.

🚴

Bike Rental

Several vendors rent bikes and helmets. Helmets are required by law. Great for Taniti City and Merriton Landing.

🚐

Hotel Shuttle

Many hotels offer shuttle services to key attractions and the airport. Check with your accommodation.

👟

On Foot

Taniti City and Merriton Landing are both flat, safe, and very walkable — the easiest way to explore.

Good to Know

Practical Information

The stuff worth knowing before you land.

💵 Currency

Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Euros and yen are accepted at many businesses. Major credit cards are widely accepted. Several banks offer currency exchange.

🔌 Power

Power outlets are 120 volts — the same as in the United States. No adapter needed for U.S. devices.

🍺 Alcohol

Alcohol may not be served or sold between midnight and 9:00 a.m. The drinking age is 18. ID may be required.

🗣️ Language

Many younger Tanitians speak fluent English. English is less common in rural areas among older residents. The hospital has multilingual staff.

🏥 Health & Safety

There is one hospital and several clinics on the island. Violent crime is very rare. As tourism grows, be mindful of pickpocketing in crowded areas.

📅 Public Holidays

Taniti observes many national holidays. Tourist attractions and restaurants may be closed on these days — plan accordingly and check ahead.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to what visitors ask most.

How do I get to Taniti? ▾
Most visitors arrive by air at Taniti's airport, which accommodates small jets and propeller planes. A small cruise ship also docks at Yellow Leaf Bay harbor once per week.
What currency does Taniti use? ▾
Taniti uses the U.S. dollar. Many businesses also accept euros and yen. Major credit cards are widely accepted, and banks offer currency exchange services.
Is Taniti safe for tourists? ▾
Taniti is generally very safe. Violent crime is rare. As tourism increases, petty crimes like pickpocketing have been reported in tourist areas, so take standard precautions with your belongings.
Do I need a power adapter? ▾
No adapter is needed for U.S. devices. Taniti uses 120-volt outlets, the same standard as the United States.
What is the best way to get around the island? ▾
Taniti City and Merriton Landing are flat and very walkable. For exploring the whole island, renting a car is your best option. Public buses run in Taniti City from 5 a.m. to 11 p.m. Taxis and bike rentals are also available.
